# 🔮 Awakened Weapons Calculator

Calculate costs, fees, attunement points, and strain when awakening and modifying weapons in Albion Online.

The bot supports the complete catalog of **136 awakenable weapons** (.4 pristine weapons) with multi-language search, live tier and server switching, and price queries via the **Albion Online Data Project**.

---

## 🔍 Commands Overview

### 1. Base Awakening Cost: `/albion_awakened base_cost [weapon] [server]`
Calculates the base cost required to awaken a pristine weapon at the Foundry:
* **Pristine Weapon Price:** Fetches recent market price for the specified weapon and server region.
* **Energy Costs:** Siphoned Energy and Avalonian Energy requirements.
* **Foundry Fee:** Silver fee required by the Foundry to awaken the weapon.
* **Total Estimated Cost:** Sum of weapon price, energy materials, and silver fee.
* **Interactive Controls:** Switch servers (Americas, Europe, Asia) or tiers (4.4 to 8.4) with one click on the embed buttons.

### 2. Attunement & Trait Calculator: `/albion_awakened attunement_cost`
Launches an interactive attunement calculator with modal controls:
* **Trait Modification:** Calculate the silver and strain required when adding, rolling, or upgrading traits.
* **Strain & Point Tracking:** Track accumulated strain and maximum potential points.
* **Buyer Reattunement:** Estimate the silver reattunement fee if purchasing an already-awakened weapon from another player.
* **Live Search:** Use the **🔍 Search Weapon** button to find any awakenable weapon in your language.
